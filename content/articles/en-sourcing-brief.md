A useful sourcing inquiry is not necessarily a long one. What matters is whether a potential supplier can understand **what you need**, **where it is going**, and **how it will be evaluated**.

## Start with the product

Share the product name, intended use, material or composition, important dimensions, and any relevant reference standard. Photos and existing specifications often help avoid misunderstandings.

## Quantities and delivery

- State the approximate order volume and whether it is a trial shipment or repeat requirement.
- Include the destination country and preferred delivery terms, if known.
- Mention your expected timeline without assuming immediate availability.
- Ask what samples or documentation can realistically be supplied.

## Clarify what is conditional

Product availability, quality assurance, export eligibility, shipping routes, and commercial terms should be verified for each order. An initial discussion is a starting point, **not a confirmed quotation**.

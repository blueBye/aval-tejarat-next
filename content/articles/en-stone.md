The appearance of natural stone is important, but commercial comparisons require far more than a photograph.

## Make the specification measurable

Identify the stone type, preferred color range, finish, thickness, dimensions, tolerances, and intended application. Different quarry blocks and production batches may vary visibly.

## Questions for a potential supplier

1. Can representative samples be provided for inspection?
2. Which test documents or quality records are available?
3. What cutting and finishing tolerances are realistic?
4. How would the material be packed for the intended route?
5. Which quantities and lead times can be confirmed in writing?

## Build your decision on verified details

Availability and export conditions are specific to the supplier, product, destination, and transaction. It is better to validate these details early than to compare offers that are not equivalent.

A professional stone quote should allow the technical team to evaluate more than colour and price. Test reports are useful only when the **test method, sample identification and intended application** are clear.

## Common performance indicators

**Water absorption and bulk specific gravity:** ASTM C97 is one referenced test method for dimension stone. Absorption can be relevant to staining, exposure and long-term detailing, although one number never determines suitability by itself.

**Flexural strength:** A test such as ASTM C880 can help the project engineer evaluate stone panels under bending loads. It does not replace a full cladding design or anchorage calculation.

**Modulus of rupture:** ASTM C99 is another listed stone test method. The project specification should say which test and value are required rather than assuming all stones need the same threshold.

**Abrasion and surface performance:** For heavy traffic floors or outdoor paving, appropriate wear and slip testing may be necessary, alongside attention to the installed finish.

## Avoid misleading comparisons

- Confirm the report refers to the **specific stone variety** being offered.
- Record sample source, dimensions, laboratory and date.
- Compare values obtained using **the same** test standards and units.
- Ask whether filling, resin treatment or surface finishing affects the applicable result.
- Verify project-specific local code and acceptance criteria with the design team.

## A simple request to send

> Please provide the stone identification, current representative samples, available absorption and strength reports, test standard references, manufacturing tolerances and any known limitations for the intended application.

Avoid making a universal pass/fail judgment based on a single metric. The correct decision is a combination of engineering, aesthetics, installation and service conditions.

The same stone can look surprisingly different after finishing. A finish is not merely a stylistic choice: it changes reflection, perceived colour, cleaning effort and sometimes surface performance.

## Three useful finish families

**Polished** surfaces emphasize reflected light and stone pattern. They are popular for feature walls and other decorative locations, but a glossy appearance should not be taken as evidence of suitable slip performance.

**Honed** surfaces are comparatively matte or satin. They can make veining feel quieter and reduce glare, but their performance still depends on material and the specific processing.

**Textured** finishes include sandblasted, bush-hammered, brushed and certain flamed treatments. They may be considered where grip or visual texture matters, but each stone and finish needs testing for the actual application.

## What buyers should request

- Finish name **and** a representative physical sample.
- Photos taken in natural light and in the intended lighting conditions.
- Surface-performance results where required, especially on wet or public walking areas.
- Confirmation of whether resin, filler, sealer or other treatments will be applied.
- Agreement on acceptable sheen, edge detail and finishing consistency.

## Do not separate finish from installation

Exterior paving, pool surrounds, indoor lobbies and wall cladding have different requirements. The relevant slip criteria, drainage, cleaning program and local codes should be determined by the project team.

For a meaningful quotation, ask suppliers to quote the **same stone, same dimensions and same finish**. A difference in finish can materially change fabrication time and cost.
